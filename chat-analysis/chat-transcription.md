# Client Project Chat Transcript 7-Question Extractor

> **Domain:** 2. Meetings, Collaboration & Communications | **Category:** `chat-analysis` | **Tier:** 🟢 Quick  
> **Use When:** Extracting key project delivery signals (milestones, new team members, challenges, action items, client feedback, technical issues, and upcoming deadlines) from a team or client chat transcript.  
> **Input:** Project chat transcript (MS Teams, Slack, etc.) | **Output:** Structured answers to 7 core project health questions  
> **Target / Tested With:** Any LLM · See also [`../meetings/summarise-msteams-meeting.md`](../meetings/summarise-msteams-meeting.md)

<details>
<summary>💬 Project Chat Transcript 7-Question Prompt</summary>

```text

1. What are the key milestones achieved in the project for the client?
2. Who are the new team members that joined the project, and what roles do they play?
3. What were the main challenges faced during the project, and how were they resolved?
4. What are the pending action items or tasks that need to be addressed?
5. What feedback or updates have been provided by the client regarding the project's progress?
6. What are the significant technical issues encountered, and what solutions were implemented?
7. What are the upcoming project deadlines or important events that the team should be aware of?
```
